Privacy Policy - Agnisiragu News

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Agnisiragu ("we", "us", "our") operates the Agnisiragu News mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: phone number, name, and OTP verification data used for login.
  • Content you submit: photos, videos, voice notes, and text submitted by reporters for news articles.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and app usage data for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Location information: approximate location, if permitted by you, to show relevant local news.
  • Push notification tokens: to deliver breaking news alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • To verify reporter identity and publish submitted news content.
  • To personalize news content and send notifications.
  • To process rewards and payouts for reporters (via Razorpay).
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or policy violations.

3. Voice and Media Processing

Voice notes submitted by reporters are processed using OpenAI Whisper to convert speech to text. Submitted content is also checked using automated moderation tools before being reviewed by our editorial team.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers strictly to operate the App, including:

  • Cloud storage and hosting providers (AWS S3, Cloudflare)
  • SMS/OTP providers (MSG91)
  • Payment processors (Razorpay)
  • Push notification services (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Advertising partners (Google AdMob)

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us
